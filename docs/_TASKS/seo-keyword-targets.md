## The filter to apply

Two things decide whether a term is worth writing for:

1. **Does it match live-event catering?** We walk between guests and make a portion per
   person, minimum 50 portions, from €425. We are not a delivery service.
2. **Does the searcher have an event?** "Hoeveel hapjes per persoon" and "borrelhapjes
   goedkoop" are people shopping at the supermarket for their own party. They will not book.

The data backs this up. `hapjes bestellen hilversum` sat at **position 4.4 on 136
impressions and got literally zero clicks** in 90 days. Position 4 with a 0% CTR is what an
intent mismatch looks like: the searcher wants a tray delivered to their door today, and our
snippet offers a live act from €425. A chunk of the wishlist below has that same problem, so
it is tiered rather than listed flat.

## Tier 4 — do not write for these

Not because they lack volume, but because the volume is the wrong people. Documented here so
we don't rediscover them in six months and think they look promising.

| Term                                                                         | Why not                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `hapjes bestellen` / `borrelhapjes bestellen` / `snacks bestellen`           | Delivery intent. `hapjes bestellen hilversum`: pos 4.4, 136 imp, **0 clicks**. We already rank and it earns nothing                                                                                          |
| `catering` / `snacks` / `borrel` (bare head terms)                           | National head terms owned by delivery platforms. No realistic path and no intent match                                                                                                                       |
| `hapjes borrel/verjaardag`                                                   | Same DIY-host segment as `/blog/hoeveel-hapjes-per-persoon`, which pulls **2,742 impressions at 0.51% CTR**. Volume without buyers                                                                           |
| `pizza catering`                                                             | We don't do pizza. Would be a lie                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `bruidstaart maken`                                                          | DIY-baker intent. They're making it themselves, not hiring us. (`wat kost een bruidstaart` is the buyer version of this)                                                                                     |
| `walking dinner` / `live cooking` (bare)                                     | **Added 16 Oct.** 2,400 and 1,300/mo, but the SERPs are culinary walking tours and Van der Valk live-cooking buffets. Wrong product behind the volume. `walking dinner bruiloft` (30) is the fitting version |

ijsfietsen, live cooking, oesterbar, sushi-chef aan huis). Naming them on `/catering/hilversum`
would be keyword farming with an intent mismatch, and Het Oestermeisje is a real brand. In a

picking a concept for a bedrijfsfeest actually thinks.

  what it costs, and where we fit. Be fair about the others. A comparison that only flatters
  the author converts nobody and Google discounts it.
- **Why it can rank**: nobody in NL owns "originele catering bedrijfsfeest" as a category
  explainer, and we already rank **pos 9.8 for `entertainend`**, so the category vocabulary
  works for us.
