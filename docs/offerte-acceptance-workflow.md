# Offerte acceptance workflow

Internal working doc for Hangende Hapjes. Goal: after a client says yes, collect everything needed to be on time with food, equipment, setup, ceremony flow, and payment.

This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Have the terms checked before putting them in a public acceptance flow.

## Recommended flow

1. Send offerte PDF with a clear valid-until date.
2. Client accepts via an acceptance link or by replying akkoord.
3. Client agrees to the terms and pays the aanbetaling.
4. Client receives the operations questionnaire.
5. Client forwards the venue/ceremoniemeester handoff to the location or contact person.
6. One week before the event, confirm final guest count, timing, dietary changes, contact person, and arrival instructions.

## What to collect after acceptance

### Client and decision maker

- Full names of the couple/client.
- Email address for planning.
- Phone number reachable on the event day.
- Invoice details if different from the contact details.
- Who has final decision authority on the day if the couple/client is unavailable.

### Event basics

- Event date.
- Location name.
- Full address.
- Indoor, outdoor, or mixed.
- Expected guest count.
- Final guest count deadline.
- Type of service: hangende hapjes, tiramisu, burrata, bruidstaart, millefoglie, or combination.
- Exact service moment: borrel, dessert, taartmoment, evening party, ceremony-adjacent moment.
- Desired serving time and latest acceptable serving time.
- Event language/tone if relevant: formal, casual, wedding, corporate, family.

### Timeline

- Guest arrival time.
- Ceremony time.
- Reception/borrel start.
- Dinner start.
- Speeches or planned pauses.
- Cake/dessert/hapjes moment.
- End time of our service.
- Any moments where no walking/service is allowed.
- Whether the timeline is fixed or flexible.

### Location access

- Earliest arrival time for us.
- Loading/unloading address.
- Parking instructions.
- Distance from parking/loading area to setup spot.
- Stairs, elevators, gravel, grass, narrow paths, or other access constraints.
- Contact person at venue with phone number.
- Whether we need to report to reception/security.
- Whether there are time slots for supplier arrival.

### Setup spot

- Where we can place coolboxes, prep materials, or cake/tiramisu setup.
- Whether the spot is shaded/cool.
- Whether guests can see the setup.
- Whether there is a back-of-house area.
- Whether there is a sturdy table available.
- Table size and height if venue supplies it.
- Whether we need to bring a table, table linen, display items, or decor.
- Whether open food can be staged there.

### Facilities

- Power availability and distance to the setup spot.
- Water tap availability.
- Sink availability.
- Fridge or cool room availability, if any.
- Waste disposal rules.
- Whether the venue allows external catering.
- Any venue rules around allergens, supplier certificates, or insurance.
- Any external catering, kitchen, corkage, service, or schotelgeld fees.

### Food and service details

- Final number of portions.
- Preferred mix/flavours.
- Dietary requirements and allergies.
- Serious allergies requiring separation or extra handling.
- Kids portions or alcohol-free versions if relevant.
- Whether the venue/client supplies plates, forks, napkins, coupe glasses, etc.
- Whether we bring disposables/eetgerij.
- Whether service is walk-around, at a table, staged, or handover to venue staff.
- Who announces or triggers the moment.

### Weather and risk

- Outdoor backup plan.
- Shade/tent availability.
- Indoor backup space.
- Bad-weather decision deadline.
- Heat-sensitive timing constraints.
- Whether the venue has a cool holding area.

### Contacts

- Client day-of contact.
- Ceremoniemeester contact.
- Venue manager contact.
- Photographer/videographer contact if they need the service moment.
- Wedding planner/contact person if applicable.

### Payment and admin

- Accepted offerte version/reference.
- Total amount incl. btw.
- Aanbetaling amount.
- Payment status.
- Remaining balance and due date.
- Final invoice details.
- Whether the date is in option, confirmed, or fully paid.

## Terms to show before payment

Use this as a draft for an acceptance checkbox. Keep it short in the UI and link to the full conditions.

Checkbox text:

Ik ga akkoord met de offerte, de praktische voorwaarden en de aanbetaling. Ik begrijp dat de boeking pas definitief is nadat Hangende Hapjes de aanbetaling heeft ontvangen.

Short version on acceptance page:

- De offerte is geldig tot en met de datum die op de offerte staat.
- De datum is pas definitief gereserveerd na akkoord en ontvangst van de aanbetaling.
- De aanbetaling wordt verrekend met het totaalbedrag.
- Het resterende bedrag wordt uiterlijk [14 dagen voor het event / op factuurdatum + 14 dagen] betaald.
- Het definitieve aantal gasten en dieetwensen worden uiterlijk [7/14] dagen voor het event doorgegeven.
- Wijzigingen na die datum zijn alleen mogelijk als inkoop en planning dit toelaten.
- Bij annulering door de klant kunnen gemaakte kosten en gereserveerde capaciteit in rekening worden gebracht.
- Als de locatie, planning, toegang, stroom/water of weersituatie wezenlijk afwijkt van de opgegeven informatie, kunnen extra kosten of aanpassingen nodig zijn.
- Bij buitenlocaties is een realistisch slechtweerplan verplicht voor producten die koel of droog moeten blijven.
- Allergenen worden zorgvuldig behandeld, maar volledige afwezigheid van kruisbesmetting kan niet worden gegarandeerd.

## Draft general terms

### 1. Offerte and booking

The offerte describes the agreed date, location, products, estimated guest count, service, price, VAT, and validity period. A booking is confirmed only after written acceptance and receipt of the required aanbetaling.

### 2. Payment

The aanbetaling is due immediately after acceptance through the provided payment link. The remaining amount is due according to the invoice or payment term stated in the offerte. Hangende Hapjes may pause preparation or release the date if payment is not received on time after reasonable reminder.

### 3. Guest count and changes

The client provides the final guest count, dietary requirements, timeline, and location details no later than the agreed deadline. Later changes are handled on a best-effort basis and may affect price, availability, or execution.

### 4. Location requirements

The client is responsible for ensuring that the location allows external catering and that supplier access, setup space, power, water, parking, and bad-weather backup are arranged where needed. Extra fees charged by the venue, including external-catering fees or schotelgeld, are for the client unless agreed otherwise in writing.

### 5. Allergens and dietary requests

Hangende Hapjes handles dietary requests carefully and asks for allergy information in advance. Because products are prepared in a working kitchen and served on location, absolute absence of traces or cross-contamination cannot be guaranteed unless expressly agreed in writing.

### 6. Cancellation by client

Cancellation must be made in writing. Costs already made, custom preparation, purchased ingredients/materials, and reserved capacity may be charged. Suggested simple schedule to review legally:

- More than 30 days before event: aanbetaling covers reserved capacity and admin.
- 14 to 30 days before event: 50% of the agreed total.
- 7 to 13 days before event: 75% of the agreed total.
- Less than 7 days before event: 100% of the agreed total.

### 7. Force majeure and safety

If execution is impossible or unsafe due to circumstances outside reasonable control, Hangende Hapjes and the client will first look for a practical alternative. If the event cannot reasonably proceed, already made costs and non-cancellable commitments may still be charged.

### 8. Photos and promotion

Hangende Hapjes may only use recognizable photos of people, private events, or venue interiors for promotion after permission from the client or rights holder.

## Best DB/frontend shape

This should eventually be in the admin database and frontend, not only in emails.

### Minimal version

Add fields to each deal:

- `accepted_terms_at`
- `accepted_terms_version`
- `deposit_amount`
- `deposit_status`
- `deposit_link`
- `final_payment_amount`
- `final_payment_status`
- `final_payment_link`
- `operations_questionnaire_sent_at`
- `operations_questionnaire_completed_at`
- `ops_json`

Use `ops_json` for the operational questionnaire first, because the questions will evolve. Split into real columns later only for fields used in calendar views, reminders, or reporting.

### Frontend pages

- `/klantportaal/[token]`: client sees quote summary, terms checkbox, accept button, payment button.
- `/klantportaal/[token]/gegevens`: questionnaire after payment/acceptance.
- Admin deal detail: status, payment state, missing ops checklist, copy buttons for ceremony/location emails.

### Admin checklist states

- Offerte sent.
- Accepted.
- Aanbetaling paid.
- Questionnaire completed.
- Venue/ceremoniemeester contacted.
- Final guest count confirmed.
- Final planning confirmed.
- Ready for prep.

## What to do now

Until the frontend exists:

1. Put the checkbox text and short terms in the offerte email.
2. Ask for a reply with "Akkoord".
3. Send payment link manually.
4. After payment, send the client questionnaire.
5. Ask them to forward the handoff email to the venue/ceremoniemeester.
6. Paste the completed details into the deal notes.
