# SEO Report: hangendehapjes.nl

_23 September 2026 · GSC: 24 August–20 September vs 27 July–23 August · Previous performance audit: 9 August; technical review: 22 September._

**Organic search is growing: clicks increased 18.4%, from 114 to 135 in equal 28-day periods. Growth is concentrated in the homepage and millefoglie page; tiramisu remains the biggest traffic source but is roughly flat. Hilversum has not yet proved the city-page strategy.** All nine inspected content pages are indexed and Google accepts their own canonical URLs. The supplied enquiry list now confirms real accepted business associated with Google and ChatGPT discovery, but the recent click increase has not yet translated into demonstrated enquiry growth.

This report completes and supersedes the access-blocked [22 September review](seo-analysis-2026-09-22.md). The production crawl findings from that review remain the technical baseline; fresh GSC performance, index inspection and sitemap reads were completed on 23 September. The owner subsequently supplied 21 enquiry/opportunity records, which are incorporated below. No website code was changed or deployed.

## Top priority actions

### 1. Progress the existing quotes and reconcile CRM status

**High · Effort: low initially · Evidence: €5,263.59 across seven open, unaccepted quotes; no expected-revenue forecast.**

**Journey:** `https://hangendehapjes.nl/#contact` → offer → acceptance. One additional €882.90 offer is already digitally accepted even though its CRM label remains “sent”; count it with accepted work.

**Fix:** check the four past-validity open quotes (€3,566.99) against their actual conversations. Two notes mention tasting, one mentions price consideration, and one already records two reminders. Give interested buyers a concrete next step, revalidate terms where appropriate, or close inactive opportunities. Do not indiscriminately send more reminders, discount all quotes or release dates. Record the next action/date and genuine loss reason. All seven open events are in 2027, so a longer decision cycle is plausible.

This is the most immediate commercial work available from the supplied data; it does not guarantee additional bookings. No messages or CRM changes were made during the audit.

### 2. Protect tiramisu's strong rankings and convert its existing audience

**High · Effort: medium · Evidence: 75 clicks per 28 days; 17 supplied opportunities involve tiramisu, including all three accepted/completed records.**

**URL:** `https://hangendehapjes.nl/blog/tiramisu-bruiloft`.

**Evidence:** 74 → 75 clicks; impressions 534 → 668; page CTR 13.86% → 11.23%. Yet `tiramisu bruidstaart` remains strong at position 1.44, 8/32 clicks/impressions, and `tiramisu bruiloft` at position 1.68, 8/19. The page-wide average of 5.35 includes other searches; it does not mean the core wedding query ranks fifth. The buying query `tiramisu taart bestellen` has expanded from 4 to 44 impressions and now earns 4 clicks at position 7.20.

**Commercial evidence:** six open tiramisu quotes total €4,601.41. The supplied product mix supports prioritizing this buying journey ahead of the smaller millefoglie click opportunity. Neither the exact landing page nor organic attribution is recorded for these leads.

**Fix:** coordinate with the existing photography/layout work. Show the real cake and walking-portion options, guest-count pricing, location coverage and a clear date-enquiry action. Add one genuine wedding example with guest count and timing. Improve an existing contextual link to `tiramisu bruidstaart` where natural; these links already exist, so this is refinement rather than a missing-link campaign. Verify the existing Umami CTA → form-start → successful-submit journey and join genuine enquiries to bookings where possible.

Do not chase the competitor-branded `tiramisu bar` term or promise a glass tower you do not provide. `tiramisu toren bruiloft` fell from 7 clicks to 0 on 27 impressions in both periods, with position 3.0 → 4.11. Monitor it, but do not distort the product to regain a tiny, partially mismatched audience. The overall page has not lost clicks.

**Persona:** Het Bruidspaar; `tiramisu bruidstaart`, `bruiloft`, `ter plekke`, `vers afgemaakt`. The prior generic “+23 clicks/month at position three” estimate is withdrawn: the core queries already outperform that generic CTR assumption.

### 3. Improve millefoglie's wedding-intent search result and enquiry path

**High · Effort: low/medium · Scenario: approximately 2–4 extra clicks per 28 days on the two measured wedding queries, not a forecast.**

**URL:** `https://hangendehapjes.nl/blog/italiaanse-bruidstaart`.

Together, 107 impressions produce 3 clicks. An illustrative combined CTR of 5–7% would produce 5–7 clicks, an additional 2–4. Counts are small and rank, device and SERP layout differ, so this is an opportunity scale, not a promised outcome.

**Fix:** keep `Millefoglie: de Italiaanse bruidstaart | Hangende Hapjes` while collecting more evidence. Test a more specific description: `Millefoglie als bruidstaart, ter plekke vers afgemaakt. Vanaf 25 gasten. Bekijk foto's en prijzen en stuur ons jullie trouwdatum.` Add an authentic wedding example and make the existing price/guest-count section easy to reach. Distinguish the actual offer from recipes and pastry definitions.

**Persona:** Het Bruidspaar. Use `millefoglie`, `italiaanse bruidstaart`, `ter plekke` and `vers afgemaakt`. Success is qualified wedding enquiries, with query-level CTR as a supporting metric.

### 5. Correct dessert schema and content dates without inventing a ranking emergency

**Medium · Effort: low/medium · Exposure: tiramisu and millefoglie together receive 99/135 current clicks; recovery forecast unavailable.**

**URLs:** `/blog/italiaanse-bruidstaart`, `/blog/tiramisu-bruiloft`, `/blog/bruidstaart`, `/blog/burrata-catering`.

**Evidence:** the 22 September crawl shows tiramisu and millefoglie using the same `/#service-toetjes` identity for distinct products and prices. Millefoglie inherits a review explicitly about tiramisu. Classic cake shares this pattern in source. Burrata uses the deployment date as its publication date; blog modification dates and sitemap lastmod values also track builds.

**Fix:** give each dessert offer a distinct stable ID and update Article references. Use real publication/substantial-update dates and complete author identity fields. These correct meaning and eligibility; they are not evidence that Google has penalized the site. ## Supporting findings

### Millefoglie title assessment: reach improved; CTR benefit unproven

Broad `millefoglie` impressions rose **150 → 546** in consecutive 28-day periods at almost unchanged position **9.13 → 9.06**. It produced just **0 → 2 clicks**. Those additional 396 impressions represent about 90% of the page's 438 additional impressions, explaining much of the falling page CTR. Broad pastry interest is not interchangeable with wedding purchase intent.

The earlier clean pre-change window (9 July–5 August) versus current period gives another useful check:

| Same query/page           | Pre-change position | Current position | Pre-change → current clicks/impressions |
| ------------------------- | ------------------: | ---------------: | --------------------------------------: |
| `millefoglie bruidstaart` |                5.61 |             2.26 |                             2/44 → 2/46 |
| `italiaanse bruidstaart`  |                5.07 |             4.30 |                             0/41 → 1/61 |
| `millefoglie`             |                9.02 |             9.06 |                           1/101 → 2/546 |

Country/device checks reinforce the caution. For `millefoglie bruidstaart` on Netherlands desktop, position improved 6.0 → 2.0 but clicks were 1/28 → 0/34. The data shows better rankings for some commercial terms, not a demonstrated CTR win. Other content changes, search demand and the small samples prevent attributing causality to the title alone. Do not revert a relevant title because aggregate CTR stayed below 3.5%.

### Indexing and sitemap: healthy

All **nine content URLs** returned PASS / submitted-and-indexed, with successful mobile Googlebot fetches and matching Google/user canonical URLs. Latest reported crawls range from **13–22 September**. There is no evidence of an indexing block causing Hilversum's weak traffic.

Google last downloaded the ten-URL sitemap on **18 September** and reports **zero errors and zero warnings**. Five indexed URLs lack a per-URL sitemap reference in Inspection; Google's response does not guarantee an exhaustive sitemap list, and these URLs are present in the live sitemap. No resubmission is warranted from this alone. [Inspection field definitions](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/urlInspection.index/UrlInspectionResult).

Rich results pass on the commercial pages, with noncritical `shippingDetails`/`hasMerchantReturnPolicy` warnings on Product markup. Do not fabricate shipping/return policies for a bespoke catering service simply to remove warnings. Accurate entity and review representation is the useful correction. `VERDICT_UNSPECIFIED` for mobile usability is not a mobile usability pass or a Core Web Vitals result.

### Do not follow the automated consolidation suggestion

The helper named `/#products` as the “winner” for local searches and suggested redirecting the homepage/location pages to it. That is a homepage fragment, not an independent page. The recommendation is rejected. Google selects the intended canonicals, and homepage/local-page impressions can coexist. Keep both while improving local relevance and proof; no evidence establishes that forced consolidation would increase qualified enquiries.

### Content gaps and what to defer

No new keyword meets the audit's strict 11–30-position, over-200-impression threshold without an existing relevant page. `bruidstaart 25 personen` and classic-cake variants have only 11 impressions each over 91 days; `bruidstaart 50 personen` has four. Add concise, truthful guest-count answers to the existing cake page instead of launching several thin pages. The original cake-cost article idea remains plausible but is not a newly validated high-volume opportunity.

### Remaining limits

PageSpeed returned quota errors on all ten attempts on 22 September. No new Lighthouse or CrUX metrics are available; the Google login does not by itself fix PageSpeed quota. The owner-supplied opportunity snapshot now supports the accepted/open values above. No new Umami session data, invoice payments or first-touch landing-page attribution were retrieved, so organic conversion rate, collected revenue and page-level ROI remain unmeasured. Keep the [22 September technical findings](seo-analysis-2026-09-22.md#additional-structured-data-fixes) and their evidence, without treating them as proof of lost ranking.

## What to ignore for now

- Generic delivery/DIY queries, competitor-branded tiramisu searches and the broad `millefoglie` CTR as standalone success targets.
- More city pages, a generic “push all pages into top three” campaign, or major title changes on the already strong tiramisu wedding queries.
- Merchant-policy warnings and more FAQ/HowTo markup as substitutes for accurate content, proof and booking measurement.
