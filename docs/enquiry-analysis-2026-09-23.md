## Counting rules

- **21 opportunity records, approximately 20 distinct clients.** Two records share the same customer and event date: an accepted tiramisu cake and a rejected Italian-lunch add-on. Keep both opportunities when reviewing products/quotes, but do not count the second as a newly acquired customer.
- **Three accepted/completed opportunities:** two marked completed and one explicitly digitally accepted, despite its CRM status still saying “Offerte verstuurd.” This is an accepted signal requiring CRM reconciliation, not proof that a deposit or invoice has been paid.
- **Seven open, unaccepted quotes.** The digitally accepted quote is excluded from this open pool.
- Amounts are **quote values as supplied**, not verified collected revenue, profit, deposits or tax-exclusive sales. VAT basis was not provided. Two records have no quote amount; one has €0.00. Missing amounts are not treated as zero.
- Source labels are self-reported. “Google” does not distinguish organic from paid search, brand searches from generic discovery, or the landing page. A TikTok → Google journey and a Google → relative referral retain both touchpoints.
- Product counts follow the product field. Two older tiramisu records do not specify cake versus walking portions. Notes can lag the current offer; verify product/status before operational action.
- Customer emails, telephone numbers, names and venue addresses are omitted from the persisted [normalized evidence](seo/2026-09-23/enquiry-analysis.json). Row IDs follow the supplied order.

The prior site crawl also found a Stip burrata-event testimonial that cannot be identified in these rows. This list can be complete for recorded enquiries without necessarily covering every direct/offline event. Reconcile that event before treating the figures as total business revenue or concluding that burrata has never sold; no additional opportunity has been invented here.

**Clicks grew 18.4%; observed enquiries did not grow in those same windows.** The difference is just two opportunities, so it cannot establish a conversion problem or a lasting decline. Two further enquiries arrived on **21 and 22 September**, after the SEO comparison cutoff; both remain open and one explicitly mentions Google.

Do not divide the 13 lifetime Google mentions by 135 recent organic clicks. Even the matched-window enquiry/click ratios would mix self-reported source, possible paid/assisted journeys, returning visits and conversion delays. An organic session-to-enquiry conversion rate still requires linked Umami or form/deal attribution.

Monthly opportunity receipts were **May 4, June 4, July 5, August 5, September 3 through the 22nd**. This looks like a small, fairly steady pipeline rather than a demonstrated surge. July includes the second opportunity for an existing customer. September is incomplete.
