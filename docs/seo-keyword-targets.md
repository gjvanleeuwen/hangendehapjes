# Keyword targets — reference list

Raw wishlist from Gijs (2026-08-09), sorted into tiers and annotated with real GSC data
from the 90-day window **2026-05-08 → 2026-08-06** (252 clicks / 5,690 impressions).

This is a **reference list for future content**, not a backlog. Pick from it when scoping a
post; don't treat every line as a task. The dated, per-post briefs live in
[seo-content-plan.md](seo-content-plan.md) and the active tasks in [../todo.md](../todo.md).

## The filter to apply

Two things decide whether a term is worth writing for:

1. **Does it match live-event catering?** We walk between guests and make a portion per
   person, minimum 50 portions, from €425. We are not a delivery service.
2. **Does the searcher have an event?** "Hoeveel hapjes per persoon" and "borrelhapjes
   goedkoop" are people shopping at the supermarket for their own party. They will not book.

The data backs this up. `hapjes bestellen hilversum` sat at **position 4.4 on 136
impressions and got literally zero clicks** in 90 days. Position 4 with a 0% CTR is what an
intent mismatch looks like: the searcher wants a tray delivered to their door today, and our
snippet offers a live act from €425. A chunk of the wishlist below has that same problem, so
it is tiered rather than listed flat.

## Tier 3 — city pages (gated on the Hilversum page proving itself)

`/catering/hilversum` shipped **2026-07-01**. In its first five weeks it went from 61 to 526
impressions but stayed flat at position ~36. It is still being evaluated by Google. **Do not
build Amsterdam / Utrecht / Rotterdam / Den Haag until Hilversum breaks into the top 20** —
otherwise we build four more pages on a pattern we haven't proven, and thin city-swap pages
are exactly what Google treats as doorway pages.

## Tier 4 — do not write for these

Not because they lack volume, but because the volume is the wrong people. Documented here so
we don't rediscover them in six months and think they look promising.

| Term                                                               | Why not                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `hapjes bestellen` / `borrelhapjes bestellen` / `snacks bestellen` | Delivery intent. `hapjes bestellen hilversum`: pos 4.4, 136 imp, **0 clicks**. We already rank and it earns nothing                |
| `catering` / `snacks` / `borrel` (bare head terms)                 | National head terms owned by delivery platforms. No realistic path and no intent match                                             |
| `hapjes borrel/verjaardag`                                         | Same DIY-host segment as `/blog/hoeveel-hapjes-per-persoon`, which pulls **2,742 impressions at 0.51% CTR**. Volume without buyers |
| `pizza catering`                                                   | We don't do pizza. Would be a lie                                                                                                  |
| `bruidstaart maken`                                                | DIY-baker intent. They're making it themselves, not hiring us. (`wat kost een bruidstaart` is the buyer version of this)           |

## Planned post: "Originele cateringideeën voor je bedrijfsfeest"

Logged 2026-08-09. This is the **right home for competitor-concept references** (oestermeisje,
ijsfietsen, live cooking, oesterbar, sushi-chef aan huis). Naming them on `/catering/hilversum`
would be keyword farming with an intent mismatch, and Het Oestermeisje is a real brand. In a
buyer-help listicle it's honest and genuinely useful, because that's exactly how someone
picking a concept for a bedrijfsfeest actually thinks.

- **Angle**: an overview of live-catering concepts you can book in NL, what each suits, roughly
  what it costs, and where we fit. Be fair about the others. A comparison that only flatters
  the author converts nobody and Google discounts it.
- **Why it can rank**: nobody in NL owns "originele catering bedrijfsfeest" as a category
  explainer, and we already rank **pos 9.8 for `entertainend`**, so the category vocabulary
  works for us.
- **Category terms to own** (these, not competitor brand names): `live catering`,
  `live cooking`, `food entertainment`, `walking act`, `originele catering bedrijfsfeest`.
- **Supporting GSC signal**: `bedrijfsfeest hilversum` 50 imp at pos 49.6, `zakelijke borrel
rijen` pos 39.3, and one literal query for _"zoek aanbieders van italiaanse hapjes voor
  zakelijke evenementen in hilversum."_ at pos 11.
- **Links to**: `/catering/hilversum` (new business section) and `/blog/burrata-catering`.
- **Care needed**: describe competitors accurately and neutrally. No claim of association, no
  disparagement.

## What this list can't tell us

GSC only shows terms we **already rank for**, so every "no impressions yet" above is unmeasured,
not zero. To get real volume numbers (especially for the Tier 1 terms we have no data on, and
for the millefoglie-vs-millefeuille question), run `/keyword-research` with these seeds:

`bruidstaart kosten`, `italiaanse hapjes bruiloft`, `catering op locatie`, `event catering
Nederland`, `hapjes receptie`
